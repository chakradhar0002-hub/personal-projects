ADDENDUM TO PLAN.txt: KEEP AWAY FROM WEEKENDS AND HOLIDAYS  (written before any outcome of this version was computed)

User request: "Don't check near the weekend or holidays."
Everything in PLAN.txt applies (MAIN sample, exclusions, signal, outcomes, same-day peers, inference, confirmation rule),
restricted as follows. Calendar = the NSE session list; a holiday is a weekday with no session; a weekend session
(Muhurat trading, special Saturday sessions) counts as a break, never as a normal session.
- NO-BREAK: day0 (the session before day1, whose close starts day1's return), day1, day2 and day3 fall on four
  consecutive calendar days, all Monday to Friday: no weekend and no holiday anywhere from day0 to day3. Day3 is then a
  Thursday (day0 Monday) or a Friday (day0 Tuesday).
- NOT-NEAR (headline): NO-BREAK, and the calendar day after day3 is also a normal weekday session (day3 is not the last
  session before a weekend or holiday). In practice day0 Monday .. day3 Thursday with the Friday open.
Tests on both: the main table (all days / after two up / after one down / after two down) and the same-day peer
difference in share down and in mean day 3 (t-test over dates, month bootstrap); first half / second half and the
CONFIRMED rule of PLAN.txt; size versions (both days below -1% and -2%); day-3 opening gap vs open-to-close; every
calendar quarter; the bounce trade and the short after 0.17% costs. Streaks longer than 2 days are not tested here (they
cannot fit inside four consecutive weekdays). Descriptive on full MAIN: the result by weekday of day3 and by whether the
window contains a weekend or a holiday.
