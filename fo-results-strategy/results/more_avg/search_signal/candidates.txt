COMMON TRADE (identical for every candidate and the baseline)
  Universe: in_fo results (tafa/C_post_results/features.csv rows, 3,280). Reaction day k = i_react.
  Entry: buy at the close of k. Exit: close of k+20. Hedge: short Nifty 50 for the same value at close k, cover close k+20.
  vsN_net = raw_H20 - nifty_H20 - 0.19   (raw_H20 = compounded stock adjusted return k+1..k+20 in %,
            nifty_H20 = (Nifty close(k+20)/close(k) - 1)*100; costs 0.17 stock + 0.02 hedge).
  raw_net = raw_H20 - 0.17 (unhedged, reported as a side figure only).
  Outcomes taken from tafa/C_post_results/trades.csv, rows with qn <= 13 ONLY (the evaluation script drops qn >= 14
  immediately after reading the file, before any arithmetic).

FEATURES (all known at the close of k or earlier). Missing value -> filter fails.
  XN            stock adjusted return on day k minus Nifty 50 return on day k, %        (features.csv)
  cut RSI14     Wilder RSI(14) at the cutoff close i_cut (2 sessions before result)     (features.csv cut_rsi14)
  vol           rx_vol_ratio_50 = volume(k) / mean volume of the prior 50 traded sessions (features.csv)

  BRK52         close(k) > highest high of the prior 250 traded sessions (k-250..k-1); needs 250 prior sessions
  LAGx          lag_pct (events_ta, at the cutoff) = 21-session stock return minus 21-session Nifty 50 return, % > x

  GOOD          features.csv GOOD: (rq PAT YoY > 25% AND rq sales YoY > 15%) OR (margin change YoY > +2pp;
                EBITDA margin for companies, net margin for financials) - reported quarter, public by day k

  PAT50         rq_pat_yoy_pct > 50
  LARGE / SMALL events.csv mcap (crore, at the cutoff) >= 100,000 / < 50,000
