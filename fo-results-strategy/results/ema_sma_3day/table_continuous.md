3,278 F&O results, 22 quarters (qn 0 = Jan-Mar 2021 results .. 21 = Apr-Jun 2026). Feature at the cutoff close; three_day = Day-1 + Result day + Day+1 returns (gross). IC = Spearman correlation inside one quarter; Mean IC = average over the quarters. t = mean / sd x sqrt(quarters). Perm p: 5,000 within-quarter shuffles, two-sided. maxT p: familywise over all 27 features. Placebo IC: same IC on ordinary F&O stock-days (no results within 10 sessions), by results season. Results - placebo: mean of the 22 quarterly differences, one-sample t-test. Confirmed = maxT p < 0.05 in q0-13 AND same sign with perm p < 0.05 in q14-21. Sorted by |Mean IC|. No feature has missing values (every result has >= 205 traded days).
