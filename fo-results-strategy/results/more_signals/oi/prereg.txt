DECISION TIME / LOOK-AHEAD
  Trade A features use data up to and including the close of the cutoff session i_cut (2 sessions before the result
  session); trade B features up to and including the close of the reaction day k = i_react. End-of-day OI / option
  closes are published after the close; as with the price features of earlier studies they are treated as known at
  the close (live OI is available intraday). Robustness (reported, not a criterion): A with all features taken one
  session earlier (i_cut - 1); B entered at the close of k+1 (hold to k+21) with the same features.

FEATURE DEFINITIONS (per stock j, session i; percent units)
  OI_i      futures open interest (shares) summed over ALL expiries (rollover-neutral), split-adjusted:
            OI_i * f_i with f_i = underlying close / adjusted close (step changes > 2% only; otherwise f constant).
  P_i       adjusted close (cumulated returns.csv).
  dOI_L(i)  (OI_i / OI_{i-L} - 1) * 100;  dP_L(i) = (P_i / P_{i-L} - 1) * 100;  L = 5, 10, and 1 (B only).
  Buildup over L (textbook, |OI change| > 10%):
            LONG BUILDUP  dP_L > 0 and dOI_L > +10      SHORT BUILDUP  dP_L < 0 and dOI_L > +10
            SHORT COVERING dP_L > 0 and dOI_L < -10     LONG UNWINDING dP_L < 0 and dOI_L < -10
  OI20(i)   OI_i / mean(OI_{i-20} .. OI_{i-1})  (>= 15 of the 20 present).
  Front contract: nearest expiry with >= 7 calendar days to expiry, else the next one (same rule for futures and
            options).
  BASIS(i)  (front futures settle / underlying close - 1) * 365 / days_to_expiry * 100 (annualised %).
  BASISDEV(i) BASIS(i) - median(BASIS_{i-60} .. BASIS_{i-1}) (>= 40 of 60 present).
  PCR(i)    total put OI / total call OI (stock options, all strikes, all expiries).  dPCR5 = PCR_i - PCR_{i-5};
            dPCR1 = PCR_i - PCR_{i-1}.
  ATM IV    front option expiry; ATM strike = listed strike nearest the underlying close (tie -> lower); call and put
            NSE settle (closing) prices; a leg is used only if it traded that day (contracts > 0); Black-Scholes,
            European, S = underlying close, T = calendar days / 365, r = 6.5%, no dividend; IV by root-finding in
            [1%, 500%]. IV = mean of the usable legs (>= 1). CIV / PIV = call / put leg IV.
  IVREL(i)  IV_i / median(IV_{i-60} .. IV_{i-1}) (>= 40 of 60 present).
  IVSPREAD(i) CIV_i - PIV_i (both legs traded; Cremers-Weinbaum call-put IV spread).
  dCIV20 = CIV_i - CIV_{i-20}; dPIV20 = PIV_i - PIV_{i-20} (An-Ang-Bali-Cakici IV innovations).
  Quintiles: "top quintile" = value > 80th percentile, "bottom" = value <= 20th percentile of the same feature over
  ALL F&O results of the same quarter (each measured at its own decision session: i_cut for A, k for B). Placebo
  days use the same cut-offs computed over the placebo days of the same group (A: qn_next; B: calendar month).
  Robustness for candidates (not a criterion): cut-offs from the previous quarter's results only (strictly point in
  time; qn 0 keeps its own).

FAMILY = the 36 tests above (A01-A22, B01-B14).

STATISTICS (per test)
  n, avg (gross signed) and net, hit rate, quarters positive (net) / quarters with trades, first 14 (qn 0-13) / last 8
  (qn 14-21): n, net, excess vs parent; excess vs parent = mean(signed pnl - signed parent mean of the same quarter);
  net without the 5 best (signed) trades.
  luck p: within-quarter permutation of the outcome over the parent pool (A: all F&O results with three_day; B: W),
  20,000 permutations each (= random same-size picks per quarter), one-sided in the test's direction.
  Holm over the 36 luck p. maxT (Westfall-Young): max over the 36 standardised statistics per permutation draw.
  Placebo: the same rule on ordinary stock-days without a result nearby.
    A: placebo_ta stock-days (in F&O, >= 10 sessions after the previous result, >= 14 before the next), forward
       3-day return k -> k+3; features at k. Placebo excess = signed (signal-days mean - all placebo days mean) per
       qn_next, weighted to the results' quarter mix. DiD = results excess - placebo excess. placebo p = share of
       10,000 draws (same per-quarter counts, from the placebo signal days) whose excess >= the results excess.
    B: quiet_days stock-days (no result session in [k-10, k+20], in F&O) that are winners (day return - Nifty > 4),
       20-session Nifty-hedged return k -> k+20 (<= 2 no-trade sessions), net of 0.19; features at k (B05: window
       ending k-2). Placebo excess = mean(signal quiet winner - all quiet winners of the same month). DiD as above;
       placebo p = share of 10,000 bootstrap means (same n, from the quiet signal winners' demeaned returns) >= the
       results excess.
  A signal WORKS only if all hold:
    (1) better than its parent in both halves (excess > 0 in first 14 and in last 8) and net > 0 in both halves;
    (2) Holm p < 0.10;
    (3) beats its placebo: DiD > 0 and placebo p < 0.10;
    (4) net without its best 5 trades > 0.
