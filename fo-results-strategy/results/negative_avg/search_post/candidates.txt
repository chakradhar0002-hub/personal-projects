FEATURE DEFINITIONS (k = i_react, the reaction-day session; all % units)
  XN        stock return on day k minus Nifty 50 return on day k (panel.XN), known at close k.
  L2/L4/L6  XN < -2 / < -4 / < -6 ("results losers").   W = XN > 4 (winners).   nonW = XN <= 4.
  RSIcut    cut_rsi14 (Wilder RSI(14) at the cutoff close i_cut).
  B50 / B200  close_k below its SMA50 / SMA200 (tafa rx_close_vs_sma50_pct < 0 / rx_close_vs_sma200_pct < 0; adjusted
            closes, traded days, incl. k).
  BRKDN_VOL close_k < lowest low of prior 20 traded sessions AND volume_k >= 1.5 x mean volume of prior 50 sessions
            (tafa BRKDN_VOL, requires >= 51 traded sessions).
  BAD       reported quarter: profit YoY < -25% OR margin change YoY < -2 pp (EBITDA margin for companies, net margin for
            banks/NBFC) (tafa BAD).  GOOD = (profit YoY > 25% and sales YoY > 15%) OR margin change > +2 pp (tafa GOOD).
            GOOD & BELOW50 = tafa GOOD and BELOW50.
  CLV_k     (close_k - low_k) / (high_k - low_k), adjusted OHLCV of day k (NaN if high = low). "Closed near the low" =
            CLV_k <= 0.25.
  SEC21_k   21-session return of the result's sector_index (events.csv mapping; Nifty 500 for unmapped industries) to
            close k: close(k)/close(k-21) - 1. "Falling sector" = SEC21_k < 0.
  DRIFT-    habitual negative drifter: n_drift_4_B >= 3 AND mean_drift_4_B < 0 (last 4 earlier results' 20-session
            post-reaction drift vs Nifty, only drifts already ended before k; panel).
  DRIFT--   strict (any reaction): n_drift_4_B = 4 AND mean_drift_4_B < 0 AND n_pos_drift_4_B <= 1.
  VSN252-   vsN_252_B < 0 (stock return over the last 252 sessions to k minus Nifty; 12-month laggard).
  SB_k      futures short build-up into k: B_dP5 < 0 AND B_dOI5 > +10 (5 sessions ending k: price down, futures OI over
            all expiries up > 10%). Uses day k's F&O bhavcopy -> tradable only from k+1 (entries E1 / E1o).
  OIUP_k    futures OI up > 5% on day k itself (B_dOI1 > 5) - fresh shorts on the down day; k's bhavcopy -> from k+1.
  DQK3      delivery quantity on day k >= 3 x its mean over k-60..k-1 (delivery file DQK); published after close k ->
            from k+1.
  FT1       day k+1 stock return minus Nifty that day < 0 (fell again the next day); known at close k+1 -> E1 only.
  SB_cut    pre-results futures short build-up at the cutoff: A_dP5 < 0 AND A_dOI5 > +10 (5 sessions to i_cut).

ENTRIES / OUTCOMES (hedged with Nifty; costs 0.17 stock + 0.02 Nifty = 0.19 per round trip)
  E0  enter at close k; exit close k+H. stock = PX(k+H)/PX(k) - 1, PX = cumulated adjusted daily returns
      (sector_lab returns.csv, blank = 0); Nifty = N(k+H)/N(k) - 1.
  E1  enter at close k+1; exit close k+1+H. Same, from k+1. Requires a trade on k+1.
  E1o enter at the open of k+1 (adjusted OHLCV open), exit close k+H (H sessions k+1..k+H).
      stock = close(k+1)/open(k+1) * PX(k+H)/PX(k+1) - 1. Nifty leg = N(k+H)/N(k) - 1 (Nifty open is not in the data;
      the hedge leg is approximated from close k - stated limitation).
  Tradable: entry-day trade exists (E0: XN known), exit session exists, <= 2 blank daily returns in the holding window.
  vsN = (stock - Nifty) * 100. SHORT PROFIT (net) = -vsN - 0.19. Raw short net (unhedged, info) = -stock*100 - 0.17.
  BASE SHORT for each (entry, H) = all tradable discovery results with that entry and H.

FINALIST RULE (fixed now, applied mechanically by search.py)
  Eligible only if ALL: (1) discovery trades >= 40 AND >= 7 discovery quarters with trades; (2) mean short profit net
  > 0 in BOTH discovery halves (qn 0-6 and qn 7-13); (3) mean short profit net minus the BASE SHORT mean net of the
  same entry and horizon >= +0.50 points.
  Rank eligible candidates by mean short profit net WITHOUT the 5 best short trades (drop the 5 biggest falls).
  Finalist 1 = top rank. Finalist 2 = the next-ranked eligible candidate whose trades (symbol, qn) overlap Finalist 1's
  in less than 50% of the smaller set (so a second slot is not spent on a horizon / entry variant of the same trades).
  At most 2. If none is eligible: no finalist (a valid answer).
  Reported, not selection inputs: luck p (random same-size picks per quarter from the base pool, 10,000 draws,
  one-sided), t across quarters, raw (unhedged) short, quarters short-profitable.
