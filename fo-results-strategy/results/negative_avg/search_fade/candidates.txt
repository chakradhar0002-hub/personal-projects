COMMON DEFINITIONS
  Data: perf/features/panel.csv (keys, i_cut, i_rd, i_p1, i_react=k, XN, lag21, cut_rsi14), tafa/C_post_results/
  features.csv (rx_rsi14, rx_gap_pct, rx_close_vs_sma50_pct, BAD, rq_pat_yoy_pct), more_signals/delivery/
  feat_results.csv (DPK, DQK), ta/build/adjusted_ohlcv.csv.gz (day-k open/high/low/close), sector_lab/data/
  {sessions,returns,index_close}.csv. Join on (symbol, qn). Session index = sessions.csv i.
  XN    = reaction-day (session k) stock return minus Nifty 50 return, % (known at close k). W = XN > 4.
  clv_k = (close_k - low_k) / (high_k - low_k) on split-adjusted OHLC of session k (NaN if high = low or not traded).
  gap_k = rx_gap_pct = open_k / previous traded close - 1, %.   C<O = close_k < open_k.
  rx_rsi14 = Wilder RSI(14) at close k.   rx_close_vs_sma50_pct = close_k / SMA50(incl. k) - 1, %.
  lag21 = 21-session stock return minus Nifty to the cutoff close i_cut, %.
  BAD   = rq_pat_yoy_pct < -25 OR margin change < -2 pp (tafa flag, reported quarter, public at the announcement).
  rq_pat_yoy_pct = reported-quarter PAT growth y/y, %.
  DPK   = delivery % on day k minus its mean over k-60..k-1 (points). DQK = delivery qty day k / mean k-60..k-1.
          Day k's delivery file is published after the close of k -> these signals enter at the close of k+1.
  r5vsN_cut = compounded stock return over sessions i_cut-4..i_cut (blank = 0, <= 1 blank) minus Nifty
          close(i_cut)/close(i_cut-5) - 1, % (known at close i_cut).

TRADE B (post-results short, Nifty-hedged). Entry e = close k (or k+1 where stated). Hold H sessions, exit close e+H.
  stock = prod(1 + r_t, t = e+1..e+H) - 1 (adjusted daily returns, blank = 0); Nifty = close(e+H)/close(e) - 1.
  SHORT PROFIT = -(stock - Nifty) x 100 - 0.19   (0.17 stock-future round trip + 0.02 Nifty hedge), %.
  Universe/tradable: panel rows with outcomes.tradable_B == True (k+21 exists, <= 2 blank sessions in k+1..k+21, XN
  known); entry k+1 additionally needs the stock to trade on k+1. Signal NaN -> not eligible.
  BASE SHORT of a window = the same short over ALL tradable discovery results with the same entry and H.

TRADE A (pre-results 3-day window). Short at close i_cut, cover at close i_p1.
  three_day = sum of the 3 daily adjusted returns (outcomes.csv); nifty3d = sum of the 3 daily Nifty 50 returns of
  sessions i_cut+1..i_p1. Raw short profit = -three_day - 0.17. Hedged short profit = -(three_day - nifty3d) - 0.19.
  BASE = the same over all discovery results with three_day.

FINALIST RULE (fixed now, before any outcome):
  1. discovery trades >= 40 AND >= 7 discovery quarters with trades;
  2. mean short profit after costs > 0 in BOTH discovery halves (qn 0-6 and qn 7-13);
  3. mean short profit after costs (all discovery trades) >= BASE short of the same window + 0.5 points;
  4. rank the survivors by mean short profit after dropping the 5 best short trades (the 5 biggest falls);
     take the top 2. If the two best are the same signal (same id prefix signal, other H / entry), the second slot goes
     to the best-ranked different signal. If none qualifies, the answer is "none".
  Reported for every candidate: n, quarters with trades, average stock return (raw and vs Nifty), mean short profit
  net, without best 5, quarters short-profitable / with trades, halves, base short of the window.
