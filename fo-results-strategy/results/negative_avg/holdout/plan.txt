RE-IMPLEMENTATION
  C12/C42: XN from sector_lab returns.csv and Nifty 50 closes; SMA50/SMA200, 20-day low, 50-day volume mean from
  ta/build/adjusted_ohlcv.csv.gz on the stock's traded days (full window required); BAD from fa_panel rq_* columns.
  Checked against tafa features.csv / panel XN (agreement reported, my flags are used).
  MA4: the feature matrix is re-built by my own merge of the source files (panel, events_ta, fa_panel, OI, delivery,
  news, dummies) and checked equal to search_model/features_A.csv; the walk-forward (pool, feature drop, clipping,
  demeaned + clipped target, GBM with the stated hyper-parameters and random_state=0, out-of-fold threshold) is my own
  code. Scored quarters 4..21: 4..13 reproduce discovery, 14..21 = holdout (training uses all completed outcomes with
  qn < q).
  Outcomes: trade B stock = cumulated adjusted daily returns k+1..k+20 (blank = 0), Nifty = N(k+20)/N(k)-1;
  tradable = k+20 exists and <= 2 blank returns in k+1..k+20 and the return at k exists. Trade A = three_day
  (outcomes.csv), recomputed from returns.csv as a check; Nifty 3-day = sum of Nifty daily returns i_cut+1..i_p1.

PRIMARY HOLDOUT MEASURES (qn 14..21)
  F1, F2: hedged short net = -(stock - Nifty)*100 - 0.19.   F3: unhedged short net = -three_day - 0.17
  (hedged -(three_day - nifty3) - 0.19 reported too).
  Per finalist: n, mean stock return raw and vs Nifty, mean short net, median, mean without the 5 biggest falls,
  quarters with trades / short-profitable; base short of the same window and quarters (all eligible F&O results);
  luck p = one-sided share of 20,000 random same-size picks per quarter from the base pool whose mean short net >=
  the finalist's (p = (1+#)/(1+20000)); Holm across the 3 finalists.
  SURVIVOR iff ALL: holdout short net > 0; short net - base short >= 0.5; Holm p < 0.10; holdout n >= 20;
  short net without the 5 biggest falls > 0.
