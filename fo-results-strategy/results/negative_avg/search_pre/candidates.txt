CANDIDATES - FAMILY "fall into results" (trade A, short the 3-day pre-results window)
Written 2026-10-11 BEFORE any outcome (three_day, d1-d3, Nifty window return, any forward return) was read or computed
in this folder. Looked at so far: column dictionaries, earlier docs, and the cutoff FEATURE table (features_cutoff.csv,
built by build_features.py, which drops every outcome column on load) with discovery feature quantiles (no outcome).
Thresholds are round / textbook values or about the discovery top/bottom quintile of the FEATURE (not of any outcome).

TRADE (every candidate): at the close of the cutoff session i_cut (2 sessions before the result session) SELL the stock
future (short), buy it back at the close of Day+1 (i_p1). Return of the stock = three_day (outcomes.csv: sum of the 3
daily adjusted returns i_cut -> i_p1, percent).
  Unhedged short profit (PRIMARY)  = -three_day - 0.17
  Nifty-hedged short profit        = -(three_day - nifty3) - 0.19, nifty3 = sum of the Nifty 50 daily returns over the
                                     same 3 sessions (i_cut+1 .. i_p1) from sector_lab index_close.csv  (REPORTED ONLY)
Universe: the 3,280 F&O results of perf/features/panel.csv (all in F&O at the time), rows with three_day.
DISCOVERY = qn 0..13 only (1,808 results). HOLDOUT qn 14..21 SEALED: outcomes are filtered to qn <= 13 on load; no
outcome / forward return of a qn >= 14 result is computed or printed. (Feature-only signal counts for qn >= 14 may be
reported.)

POINT IN TIME: price / volume / TA / sector index features use closes up to and including i_cut (the trade is at that
close, as in every earlier trade-A study). F&O bhavcopy features (OI, PCR, IV, basis) use the A1_ columns = session
i_cut-1, because the bhavcopy of day t is published after t's close. Delivery features end at e = i_cut-1. News
features count only items broadcast before 15:00 IST of the cutoff day. Fundamentals = fa_panel pre-cutoff block
(latest 4 quarters published before the cutoff). A missing feature = rule not met (NaN comparisons are False).

FINALIST RULE (stated before looking; applied to the PRIMARY unhedged short):
  (1) discovery trades >= 40 and >= 7 discovery quarters with trades;
  (2) mean unhedged short profit after costs > 0 in BOTH discovery halves (qn 0-6 and qn 7-13);
  (3) mean unhedged short profit after costs >= base short (all discovery results shorted: -mean(three_day) - 0.17)
      + 0.50 points;
  (4) rank the qualifiers by mean unhedged short profit after costs WITHOUT the 5 best short trades (the 5 biggest
      falls removed); take at most the top 2. If none qualifies, the answer is NONE.
  Also reported per candidate (not criteria): Nifty-hedged version, quarters short-profitable / quarters with trades,
  within-quarter random-pick luck p (5,000 draws, one-sided, mean three_day as low as observed) and Holm over the family.
