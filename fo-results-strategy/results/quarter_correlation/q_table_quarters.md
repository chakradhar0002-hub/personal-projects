Nifty part: Nifty 50 three-day sum on the same three sessions as each result, averaged over the quarter's results. Stock part: Q minus the Nifty part, in percentage points. Sd: cross-sectional standard deviation of the three-day sums. Best / worst sector: highest / lowest NSE index sector average among sectors with at least 4 results that quarter (number of results in brackets), as in docs/NIFTY_SECTOR_3DAY.md. Best - worst: their difference, percentage points. All 22 quarters: total results; every other column is the average of the 22 quarter values.
